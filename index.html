Stranger Call – Live Video Chat

Stranger Call – Live Video Chat is a modern social networking app that allows users to connect instantly with random people from around the world through live video and voice calls.

🔥 Key Features:
  • Random live video calling with real users
  • High-quality audio & video experience
  • Simple and fast user interface
  • Global user connectivity
  • Safe & secure call environment
⚙️ How It Works:
  • Open the app and tap on “Start Call”
  • Get instantly connected with a random user
  • Enjoy live video or voice conversation
  • End the call anytime and connect again
🔒 Safety & Privacy:
  • No personal data shared publicly
  • Users can disconnect anytime
  • Strict content and abuse policies
